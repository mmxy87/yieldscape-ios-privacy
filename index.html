What YieldScape collects

YieldScape does not require an account, and it has no servers of its own. Everything you enter — client names, email addresses, phone numbers, property addresses and gate codes, job details, materials, payments, and before/after photos — is stored only on your device. YieldScape does not send that information to us or to any server.

The only times your data leaves the device are when you choose to send it: sharing a job summary through the system share sheet, or exporting a backup file yourself. YieldScape never shares it on its own.

Advertising

YieldScape is free, with banner ads at the top and bottom of the screen. There are no subscriptions and no paywalled features.

Those ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location derived from IP address, and diagnostics to serve ads, as described in Google's published SDK disclosure. Ads are limited to general-audience content (Google's G rating). YieldScape is a business tool for adults; it is not directed at children, and no child-directed advertising treatment is set.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not offer in-app purchases. YieldScape contains no chat and no user-generated content shared with other users.

Children

YieldScape is designed for adult landscaping operators running a business. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: yieldscape.support@gmail.com.
